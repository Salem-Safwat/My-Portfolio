About Me

As mentioned, I'm Salem Safwat, a passionate grade 12 student at STEM High School for Boys - 6th of October. I have a strong interest in Astrophysics, Engineering and Computer Science, which makes me spend almost of my free time exploring these fields, learning some programming concepts, and joining science fair competitions. The remaining free time is dedicated to practicing my hobbiesand enjoying with my friends.

Being a student at STEM October has allowed me to dive deep into practical challenges through experimentations in various scientific domains inside our laboratories. Also, I'm developing my skills in coding and web development currently. My long-term goal is to get along a major in Engineering, specifically Mechanical Engineering linked with Astrophysics.

When I'm not coding or studying, you will most likely find me immersed in a book or working in my novel. Writing is a powerful outlet for me; it allows me to build worlds with words just like programming allows me to build programms or websites with code. Balancing scientific logic with creative storytelling helps me keep a fresh perspective on everything I do.

For me, productivity is about finding the right balance between hard work and meaningful connections. I love sharing ideas with my friends, engaging in deep discussions about tech and astrophysics, and constantly pushing my boundaries. I strive to make every website I build, and every story I write, a reflection of my curiosity and dedication.
